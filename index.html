Rimpy's Home A design proposal for the kitchen, dining area and living room

The rooms are not short of quality. They are short of contrast. Espresso cabinets, an espresso floor, a dark beam, dark shelving and taupe walls sit in almost the same tonal register, so the daylight that enters the house is absorbed rather than returned. Everything on the following pages follows from that single observation.
